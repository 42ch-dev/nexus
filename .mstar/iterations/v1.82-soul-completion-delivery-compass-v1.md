## 0. Context — completing the SOUL surface after V1.81

V1.81 (PR #108, 2026-07-02) shipped Creator SOUL Maturation: the world-agnostic
Creator-SOUL Narrative (on-demand LLM synthesis + stale-invalidation) + a
per-World **projection** (the world-selector drills keyword/drift/growth into a
world's fragment subset). Two SOUL gaps were explicitly flagged for follow-up:

1. **Worlds-endpoint wiring gap**: the V1.81 world-selector labels options by
   raw `world_id` because the memory/SOUL surface did not consume the worlds
   list. Codebase inspection during V1.82 iteration-start found that a worlds
   endpoint **already exists** — `/v1/local/narrative/worlds` (list) +
   `/v1/local/narrative/worlds/{world_id}` (detail) return `World` structs with
   `title` + `owner_creator_id`. So this gap is a **frontend wiring task**
   (consume the existing endpoint), not a new-endpoint build.
2. **per-World narrative deferred**: V1.81's narrative was Creator-level
   (world-agnostic) only; per-World LLM narratives were backlog.

V1.82 closes both: it **completes the SOUL surface** by (a) wiring the
world-selector to render titles + enabling the Work-backed subset-empty path
(worlds-endpoint wiring), and (b) extending the SOUL narrative to **per-World**
— the Creator SOUL's inclination within a specific world. The Creator SOUL is
the whole (world-agnostic); a per-World narrative is a subset — each has its own
narrative.

This is the natural completion of the SOUL track the user has driven across
V1.79 (Track B) → V1.81 (Maturation) → V1.82 (Completion). After V1.82 the SOUL
surface offers: Creator-level narrative + per-World narratives + worlds-endpoint
wiring (titled selector) + (V1.81) keyword/drift/growth projection.

companion hygiene sweep:

### 2.1 User stories (author persona)

**SP-1 — per-World Narrative**

- *As an author* who writes across multiple worlds, when I select a world in the
  SOUL selector I can read a narrative of who I am **within that world** — the
  themes and shifts my writing in that world has internalized — distinct from
  my Creator-level narrative, so I can reflect on how each world shapes my
  creative identity. Selecting "All worlds" shows the Creator-level narrative;
  selecting a specific world shows that world's own per-World narrative.
- *As an author*, each world's narrative is on-demand (I trigger "Reflect on
  this world's SOUL") and becomes stale when new fragments arrive for that
  world, mirroring the Creator-level behavior.
- *As an author* with insufficient data in a specific world (few fragments or
  keywords for that world), the per-World narrative surface shows the graceful
  "not enough SOUL yet in this world" state rather than a thin narrative. A
  per-World narrative is distinct and valuable when it (a) surfaces at least
  one world-specific theme or shift not prominent in the Creator whole, or (b)
  meets the same three-point quality bar (≥2 theme keywords + ≥1 temporal
  shift + forward-looking close) applied to the world's own fragments. A thin
  restatement of the Creator narrative without world signal fails the gate.

**SP-2 — Titled world selector**

- *As an author*, the world selector shows world **titles** (not opaque ids), so
  I can choose the world I want to reflect on by name.
- *As an author*, worlds I have Works in (but no fragments yet) appear in the
  selector and show an honest empty-subset state, so the projection is not
  silently limited to fragment-bearing worlds. The selector drives both the
  visualization *and* the narrative scope.
