## Architecture lock (architect Seat 2)

- **No package/API expansion:** this is a Studio presentation correction using existing assets/exports. Do not change SVG geometry, `logoVariants`, tokens, dependencies, or the `@42ch/nexus-ui` promotion list.
- **Plate previews:** `primary` and `whiteBg` are square 284×284 plate SVGs. Render them with asset-native width-fill `<img>` markup in `LogoCard`; do not force width through `<NexusLogo size>`, whose fixed inline height/auto-width contract is correct for runtime specimens.
- **Wordmark:** keep `logo-white.svg` and `logo-text.svg` unchanged. Increase only rendered wordmark height/spacing until measured painted cap-height is ≥60% of the 28px mark; retain human screenshot sign-off because jsdom cannot measure SVG painted bounds.
- **Mini shell:** one deep titlebar row owns label + logo. Remove the separate body-logo row and identical background ternary; theme variation remains in text color/body surface.
- **Residual handoff:** P1 supplies code/comment/test evidence; P3 owns ledger archival. P1 does not mutate residual lifecycle as a side effect of visual work.
