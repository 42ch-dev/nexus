## Problem

> **Architect correction (2026-07-12):** the PM compass/spec state Design Studio
> has "no canvas surface gallery". This is **factually wrong** — the gallery
> **already exists**: `SurfacesCanvasPage` at `/surfaces/canvas`
> (`apps/design-studio/src/pages/surfaces.tsx:611`) renders
> `CanvasSurfacesFixtures` (`src/fixtures/canvas-surfaces-fixtures.tsx`, 673
> lines) — a comprehensive V1.108 P1 fixture with shell chrome, dot-grid,
> controls, minimap, outline node matrix (Volume/Chapter/Timeline/Scene/Beat),
> and context-menu matrices. It is already daemon-independent, RF-free, and
> token-driven. The route + nav entry already exist (`App.tsx:60`,
> `SURFACES_SECTIONS` "Canvas").
>
> **The real gap:** the existing fixture mirrors the **Outline** surface chrome
> only. **Strategy** surface chrome (state-machine nodes, edges, inspectors) and
> **World KB** surface chrome (entity nodes, relationship edges, source anchors)
> are NOT mirrored. P2 is therefore **re-scoped from "add a Canvas page" to
> "expand the existing fixture with Strategy + World KB surface chrome"**.

Design Studio (`apps/design-studio/src/pages/surfaces.tsx`) has a Canvas surface
page (`/surfaces/canvas`) that mirrors the **Outline** surface chrome only. The
canvas has three rich surfaces (Strategy, Outline+Timeline, World KB) but only
one is previewable under studio-first. V1.108 long-term Done: "previewable in
Design Studio under studio-first".
