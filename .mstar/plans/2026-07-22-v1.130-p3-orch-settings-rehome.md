## Architecture lock

- **Hard gate:** do not dispatch until P1’s modal host is merged and P3a proves Strategy/Sessions/Modules healthy + engine-unavailable behavior. P3b contains no load-path repair.
- **Shell vs pages:** P1’s app-level Settings modal host exclusively owns chrome, sizing, focus, dismiss, dirty guard, and safe background route. Existing Settings section modules remain content-only. P3b adds section registry entries and may extract content wrappers, but must not nest or fork dialogs.
- **Compute/Modules:** reuse the existing `ModulesPage` data/query behavior as a Settings section body. The global Settings section owns Modules navigation/detail; remove Compute from 编排 only after the modal section is functional.
- **工作区:** the current profile/workspace choice surface is renamed 工作区 and mounted in 编排 功能区. Connection/path/advanced global settings stay in Settings; moving the label does not move daemon transport configuration.
- **Deep links:** `/settings`, `/settings/agent`, `/settings/appearance`, `/settings/advanced`, `/settings/workspace`, and the new Compute/Modules section resolve through one compatibility adapter into the modal section registry. Direct entry uses `/works` as safe background; in-app entry restores the prior non-settings route on close. Unknown sections fall back to the modal default, not a full-page shell.
- **Dismiss/a11y:** all close vectors use P1 `requestClose`; dirty section registrations trigger discard confirmation. Focus returns to the invoking gear/link; modal section navigation is keyboard reachable.
- **Wire:** route/IA reuse only, `wire_contracts_changed: false`.
