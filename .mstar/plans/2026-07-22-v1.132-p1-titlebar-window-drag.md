**Goal:** Dragging Chronos titlebar (including logo/title paint) moves the window; logo/title no longer image-drag or text-select.

**Architecture lock (2026-07-22):** The desktop Overlay remains window-chrome owner; the web titlebar owns visual chrome and `data-tauri-drag-region`. Logo/title paint is draggable chrome, while gear/theme/health remain explicit `no-drag` controls. Keep `draggable={false}` on the mark and `select-none` on title text. Do not replace Overlay or introduce `-webkit-app-region`; Studio is presentational and Overlay smoke is authoritative for native movement. Supersedes V1.131 empty-paint-only AC.

**Tech Stack:** `apps/web`, `apps/design-studio`, `apps/desktop` Overlay.

### Architecture lock block

- Drag coverage belongs to non-interactive titlebar chrome; controls must retain clickability through explicit no-drag islands.
- Browser/Studio behavior cannot substitute for desktop Overlay smoke; failure at that boundary is an integration defect.
- Native image dragging and text selection are presentation failures, not wire or routing concerns.
