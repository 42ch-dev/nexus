## Problem Statement

V1.114 shipped Canvas foundation (adapter + dagre + compute registry + state
read). V1.115 made it honest (adapter complete across product orchestrators +
manifest single-source). The V1.115 roadmap names three deep-dive candidates:

1. **Strategy `onConnect`** for inner-graph groups (canvas capability)
2. **Compute state editor** (compute depth — write/edit module `body.state`)
3. **5th canvas surface** (compute graph / session replay on the adapter)

Before PM picks one for V1.117+, we need an evidence-based readiness
assessment: are the foundations actually ready, or are there hidden gaps?

This plan produces **evidence and skeletons**, not a product surface.
