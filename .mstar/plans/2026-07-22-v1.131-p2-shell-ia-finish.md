## Architecture lock (architect Seat 2)

- **Single host:** one app-level `SettingsModalHost` owns the sole Radix Settings dialog, ≥80vw×80vh desktop sizing, responsive bounds, focus/scroll behavior, section navigation, URL resolver, safe background route, and dirty-source registry.
- **Typed registry:** one ordered `SettingsSectionId` registry covers `agent`, `workspace` (path configuration), `appearance`, `modules`, and `advanced`. Existing section modules are content-only; they do not own `Dialog.Root` or fork Settings chrome.
- **Route contract:** render normal routes against the last non-settings location while `/settings/*` drives the modal. Direct loads use `/works`; close restores the saved pathname/search/hash. Normalize `/settings/connection` and `/settings/setup` into Advanced hashes, `/modules` into Modules, and unknown sections into the default Agent section.
- **Host API:** titlebar/routes use one typed controller (`openSettings`, `selectSection`, `requestClose`, `registerDirtySource`, or equivalent). Every close vector passes through `requestClose`; dirty confirmation is host-owned and remains within the single Settings experience.
- **功能区 footer IA:** the 功能区 footer mode switch already exists and is the implementation baseline. Harden it and remove stale top-switch assumptions; do not build a second switch. Render `FooterProfiles` only in 编排 and label that selector **工作区**.
- **Compute:** reuse/extract the existing `ModulesPage` list/detail/query/error body as the `modules` registry section. Do not duplicate query hooks/state or introduce a nested modal. Retire `/modules` to a compatibility adapter only after modal tests pass.
- **Profile SSOT:** moving the selector changes placement only. Creator list/create/active coordination and persistence remain on existing `NexusClient`/daemon boundaries; no wire or schema change.
- **Promotion list:** no `@42ch/nexus-ui` primitive promotion. Studio consumes props-driven `@web-layout/*` and `@web-settings/*` extracts.
