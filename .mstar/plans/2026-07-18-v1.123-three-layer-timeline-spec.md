### Three-layer semantics (locked in compass; this plan canonizes them)

| Layer | Granularity | Time span | Author voice | Primary domain |
|-------|-------------|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| **Brief** | World-global | Multi-decade / era / age | "In the Age of Stars, the kingdoms rose." | **World** |
| **Narrative** | Event-level | Human-paced (days/weeks/years) | "On Midsummer's Eve, the treaty was signed." | **Shared** |
| **Moment** | Scene/beat-precise | Sub-scene (minutes/hours within a scene) | "She paused at the door, then spoke the name." | **Work** |

World Timeline composition: Brief (hero) + Narrative (peer). Moment out of scope for World.
Work Timeline composition: Narrative (peer) + Moment (hero). Brief out of scope for Work.
