- **Conflict policy:** reuses `WorldKbConflictError` (409) + `WorldKbValidationError` (422); no Timeline-specific conflict DTO.
- **Honest empty-state:** sparse World timeline (no `block_type=event` entities) is a valid MVP; empty-state copy explains the spine; `summarizeGraph` includes ordering disclaimer when temporal signals are partial/absent.
